Background

1991 — Joined Perlis State Economic Development Corporation (PKENPs)

Telephone Operator & Receptionist

Managed incoming telephone calls and public enquiries while assisting visitors and directing them to the relevant departments. Served as the front-line representative of the organization with professionalism and excellent communication skills.

2001 — Promoted to Administrative Clerk

Administrative Clerk

Handled office documentation and clerical tasks while assisting in administrative coordination and daily office operations. Supported internal department management activities efficiently.

2005 — Administration Department Staff

Administration Department Personnel

Managed administrative records and organizational procedures while assisting communication between departments and supporting staff coordination activities.

2012 — Entrepreneurship Development Division Assistant

Entrepreneurship & Community Support Staff

Assisted entrepreneurship and community development programs while supporting local business and economic growth initiatives. Also provided guidance and information to entrepreneurs and students.

2023 — Early Retirement After 32 Years of Service

Retired after more than three decades of dedicated service with PKENPs and was recognized for professionalism, commitment, and contribution to public service development in Perlis.

Present — Dataran Empangan Timah Tasoh

Boat Convoy Supervisor & Tourism Activity Coordinator

Supervises and coordinates boat convoy activities while assisting visitors and tourists at Timah Tasoh. Actively supports tourism and recreational programs, participates in community boat patrol activities, and promotes local tourism through Facebook and TikTok platforms.
