This page is about Nazrina Binti Ahmad, a dedicated staff member maintaining Dataran Empangan Timah Tasoh, Perlis.

Introduction

Puan Nazrina Binti Ahmad is a retired staff member of the Perlis State Economic Development Corporation (PKENPs), where she dedicated more than 32 years of service from 1991 until her early retirement in 2023. Throughout her career, she gained extensive experience in administration, customer service, and community engagement, contributing significantly to the development initiatives of the state of Perlis.
