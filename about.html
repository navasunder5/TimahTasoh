Background

Puan Nazrina Binti Ahmad is a retired staff member of the Perlis State Economic Development Corporation (PKENPs), where she dedicated more than 32 years of service from 1991 until her early retirement in 2023. Throughout her career, she gained extensive experience in administration, customer service, and community engagement, contributing significantly to the development initiatives of the state of Perlis. She began her career as a telephone operator and receptionist, managing incoming calls, assisting visitors, and handling front-counter services. Her role required excellent communication skills, patience, and professionalism, as she was often the first point of contact for the public. Over time, her dedication and strong work ethic earned her a promotion to clerk within the administration department, where she later served in several divisions including administration and entrepreneurship development. According to Puan Nazrina, one of the biggest challenges during her early years was managing communication with the public while maintaining professionalism under pressure. As a telephone operator, she had to respond to various types of callers, including those who were upset or demanding. Despite the challenges, she always believed in treating people politely and respectfully, as she understood the importance of maintaining a positive image for the organization. The Perlis State Economic Development Corporation (PKENPs) plays an important role in supporting the growth and development of Perlis through entrepreneurship programs, property development, and community initiatives. Puan Nazrina expressed pride in being part of an organization that contributes to the progress of the state and supports local communities, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs. Today, after retirement, Puan Nazrina remains actively involved in the local community. She currently helps manage and supervise the boat convoy activities at Dataran Empangan Timah Tasoh, one of Perlis’ popular recreational and tourism attractions. Her responsibilities include coordinating boat activities, assisting visitors, and ensuring the smooth operation of tourism-related programs around the lake area. In addition, she is actively involved with a local boat patrol and community organization that promotes tourism and recreational activities around Timah Tasoh. Visitors can also learn more about their activities through social media platforms such as Facebook and TikTok. With decades of experience in public service and community involvement, Puan Nazrina continues to contribute positively to the local tourism sector while sharing her knowledge and experience with the younger generation.
